# 黒n/LABS or KURONLABS

**From thought to system.**

KURONLABS is an independent software and digital product studio focused on turning ideas into thoughtful, functional, and scalable digital products.

We design, build, experiment, and ship.

## About KURONLABS

The name **KURON** comes from two ideas:

**Kuro (黒)** is Japanese for *black*.  
**Neuron** represents thought, ideas, and connection.

To us, black represents the unknown, the space before an idea takes shape. A neuron represents what happens next, when thoughts connect, evolve, and become something real.

**Labs** represents how we approach our work. We experiment, question, build, test, and refine until an idea becomes a working product.

That is KURONLABS.

**From thought to system.**
